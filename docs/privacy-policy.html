Stepasaurus Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Stepasaurus (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a step-powered dinosaur hatching game for Android (and a separate iOS app). This policy describes how we handle information when you use the Android app and its Wear OS companion.

Summary

  • We read step count from Health Connect only when you tap Sync Steps.
  • Game progress is stored on your device.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your steps for tracking across other apps.
  • We do not require an account or collect your email through the app.

Information we access

Steps (Health Connect) — With your permission, the app reads your step count from Health Connect to hatch eggs and grow dinosaurs. Sync happens only when you manually tap Sync Steps on the Home screen. We do not read steps automatically in the background.

We do not access location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Information stored on your device

  • Active egg / creature progress (steps, stage, species)
  • Completed creatures and collection stats
  • App preferences (e.g. developer test settings in debug builds only)

This data stays in local app storage unless you uninstall the app or clear app data.

Wear OS companion

If you install the Wear OS companion on a paired watch, your phone sends your current creature state (name, stage, progress, art keys) to the watch over Google’s Wearable Data Layer so the watch can display the same egg or dinosaur. This sync does not leave your Google account devices except as handled by Google’s platform services.

Garmin Connect IQ (optional)

Debug / experimental builds may integrate with Garmin’s companion SDK to show creature state on supported Garmin watches when Garmin Connect Mobile is installed. No step data is sent to Garmin through Stepasaurus; step counts on the phone still come from Health Connect.

What we do not do

  • No advertising or ad profiling
  • No sale or rental of personal data
  • No cross-app tracking for marketing
  • No server-side account database operated by us for normal gameplay

Children

The app is a family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above (local game state and optional Health Connect steps on the device).

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes local game data. Revoking Health Connect permission stops future step reads; previously synced steps already applied to your creature remain in local game state until you reset or uninstall.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
